## Western mode — Ragtime piano

Scott Joplin ragtime compositions (published 1899–1909) — the underlying
**musical works are Public Domain** (US, pre-1928). Files under
`western/*.mp3`. (Recording provenance not independently re-verified in this
pass; compositions are unambiguously public domain.)

## Fallout 2 & Parov Stelar modes

`fallout/*.mp3` and `parov/*.mp3` slots reference third-party commercial
soundtracks and are NOT bundled as open-source assets. These directories are
not populated with committed audio here; the modes fall back to the procedural
synth soundtrack when files are absent. They are excluded from the
commercial-safe open-source audit above.
